02Overall direction

For now the page is very classic. I’d like you to take more risks with the layouts. Here are the four main ideas:

2.1 · Alternate black and white backgrounds

I’d like more sections with a black background, like section 03, alternating with the light sections. This contrast gives rhythm and makes the page easier to read.

2.2 · One full-width blue section

I’d also like one section with a blue background (the logo blue), like the store block in v1, but spanning the full width of the screen. I’ll let you choose where on the page it has the most impact.

2.3 · Much less text

Too much information kills the information. Each section should show one strong idea: a great visual that speaks for itself, a headline and a short text. No need to detail everything every time (see comment 3.2).

Sonoo has a lot of features, so we will never be as minimal as a site like Deezer, but that’s the direction: less, but better shown.

2.4 · Bolder layouts

Visuals that break out of their frame, tilted phones and cards that overlap, text and image switching sides from one section to the next, small hand-drawn annotations… The 5 examples on the following pages show what I have in mind.

Important: these are inspiration for the mood and the way the page is composed. I don’t want to copy any of these sites.

What I like: the page is very clean (very little information), it plays with a strong black / white contrast, navigation feels nice and the curved transitions between the black and white sections are great.

I know I asked you to avoid any resemblance to Deezer for the logo: that’s still true. Here, I’m only interested in the structure and rhythm of the page, not their look.

05Imagery

I’m still not on board with the images in the guide. It’s not a big deal in itself, since I’m the only one who sees the Brand Style Guide, but it’s important that you understand the direction we’re taking, because it will guide everything else.

It may seem paradoxical: we’re building a professional platform, but the type beat market is overwhelmingly rap. We need to be professional while representing rap culture.

Here are photos that better define the image I expect. What they have in common:

  • studio or stage, at night or in low light;
  • either high-contrast black and white, or a blue / purple mood (which fits the Sonoo blue very well);
  • smoke, headphones, mics, mixing desks, DAW screens;
  • people often in profile, from behind, or with their face partly hidden;
  • candid moments, not posed; a grainy, cinematic look;
  • a real rap / trap style.

All these photos come from Pinterest. They are only meant to inspire you, not necessarily to be used as they are.
